# ONGC Overview

Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited is a public sector energy company in India with a major role in exploration and production. Publicly available information describes ONGC as a key upstream oil and gas enterprise involved in discovering, developing, and producing hydrocarbons.

## Core Functions

- Exploration of oil and gas prospects using geological, geophysical, and engineering inputs.
- Drilling and well services for appraisal and development.
- Production operations for crude oil and natural gas assets.
- Reservoir management to improve recovery and optimize field performance.
- Corporate governance, compliance, safety, sustainability, and community initiatives.

## FAQs

### What is ONGC?

ONGC is an Indian public sector energy company primarily associated with upstream oil and gas exploration and production.
