# Production Operations And Refinery Basics

## Production

Production operations bring oil and gas from wells to surface facilities while managing safety, flow assurance, separation, measurement, and asset reliability.

## Operations

Operational excellence depends on preventive maintenance, permit systems, shift handover, process monitoring, and incident reporting.

## Refinery Basics

Refining converts crude oil into useful products through separation and conversion processes. Common concepts include distillation, treating, cracking, blending, and quality control.
