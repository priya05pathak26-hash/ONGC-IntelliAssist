# Exploration Drilling And Reservoir Engineering

## Exploration

Exploration identifies prospective hydrocarbon resources using geological studies, seismic data, basin analysis, and exploratory drilling.

## Drilling

Drilling creates wellbores safely and efficiently to access subsurface formations. Key concerns include well control, mud systems, casing, cementing, and blowout prevention.

## Reservoir Engineering

Reservoir engineering estimates hydrocarbon volumes, pressure behavior, recovery mechanisms, and development strategies. It uses production data, pressure tests, geological models, and simulation.
