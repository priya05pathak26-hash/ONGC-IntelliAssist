# IT Support And Cybersecurity

## IT Support

IT support handles identity access, devices, applications, networks, email, collaboration tools, and service requests.

## Cybersecurity

- Use strong passwords and approved multi-factor authentication where available.
- Do not open suspicious attachments or links.
- Report phishing, malware alerts, lost devices, and unauthorized access immediately.
- Use approved software and avoid sharing credentials.

## Data Handling

Classify information correctly and share documents only through authorized channels.
