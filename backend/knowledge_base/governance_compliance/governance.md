# Corporate Governance Legal And Compliance

Corporate governance provides accountability, transparency, ethical conduct, and responsible decision-making.

## Compliance Practices

- Follow applicable laws, regulations, company policies, and delegated authorities.
- Maintain accurate records and approvals.
- Report conflicts of interest, fraud concerns, safety violations, and policy breaches through approved channels.

## Legal Coordination

Legal matters should be handled through authorized teams, documented instructions, and approved contract templates where applicable.
