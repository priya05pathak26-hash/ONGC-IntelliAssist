# Finance And Accounts

Finance and Accounts manages budgeting, accounting, taxation, internal controls, audits, payments, and financial reporting.

## Budgeting

- Budgets translate business plans into approved financial limits.
- Departments should track commitments, actual expenditure, and variance against approved allocations.

## Accounting

- Accounting records business transactions using approved standards, internal controls, and documented approvals.
- Reconciliation, maker-checker controls, and audit trails support financial integrity.

## Taxation

- Tax compliance includes accurate classification, timely filing, documentation, and coordination with current legal requirements.
- Tax positions should be validated with the responsible finance or tax team.

## Procurement Finance

- Vendor payments should follow purchase order terms, receipt confirmation, invoice validation, tax checks, and approval hierarchy.
