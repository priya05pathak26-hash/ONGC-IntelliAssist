# Procurement

Procurement ensures that goods and services are sourced transparently, competitively, and in line with approved delegation of powers.

## Standard Practices

- Requirement definition and technical specification.
- Budget availability confirmation.
- Tendering or approved sourcing route.
- Bid evaluation using stated criteria.
- Purchase order or contract award.
- Delivery, inspection, invoice processing, and vendor performance review.

## Vendor Governance

- Vendor selection should avoid conflict of interest.
- Procurement records should preserve traceability from requirement to payment.
- Contract deviations should be documented and approved.
