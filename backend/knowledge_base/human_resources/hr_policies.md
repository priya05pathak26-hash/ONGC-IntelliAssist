# Human Resources

Human Resources supports workforce planning, employee lifecycle management, learning, performance, welfare, and policy communication.

## Recruitment

- Recruitment in public sector enterprises normally follows approved notifications, eligibility criteria, transparent selection stages, and document verification.
- Candidates should rely on official recruitment advertisements and authorized portals.
- Common stages may include application screening, written tests, interviews, medical examination, and joining formalities.

## Employee Benefits

- Benefits usually include statutory benefits, welfare measures, medical support, retirement benefits, insurance, and allowances as defined by approved policy.
- Employees should verify benefit eligibility from the latest official circular or HR portal.

## Leave Policy

- Leave rules commonly define earned leave, casual leave, medical leave, maternity or paternity provisions, holidays, and approval workflows.
- Policy-sensitive leave questions should be answered from uploaded official policy documents when available.

## Performance Management

- Performance management aligns individual goals with department objectives.
- Typical systems include goal setting, mid-year review, annual appraisal, competency feedback, and development planning.

## Training

- Enterprise training improves technical, safety, managerial, compliance, and digital capabilities.
- Training nominations should be aligned with role requirements and business priorities.
