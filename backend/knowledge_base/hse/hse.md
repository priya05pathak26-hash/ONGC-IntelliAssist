# Health Safety And Environment

HSE protects people, assets, communities, and the environment through hazard identification, risk controls, training, emergency preparedness, and incident learning.

## Fire Safety

- Maintain clear access to fire extinguishers, hydrants, alarms, and emergency exits.
- Report hot work, fuel leaks, electrical hazards, and blocked exits immediately.
- Follow permit-to-work controls for high-risk work.

## PPE

- PPE should match the task hazard: helmets, safety shoes, gloves, goggles, face shields, hearing protection, flame-resistant clothing, or respiratory protection.
- PPE is the last line of defense and should complement engineering and administrative controls.

## Emergency Response

- Know muster points, alarms, emergency contacts, and evacuation routes.
- Stop work when conditions are unsafe.
- Report incidents and near misses promptly so corrective actions can be taken.
